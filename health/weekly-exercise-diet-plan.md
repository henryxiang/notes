# Weekly Exercise & Dietary Plan (A1C Reduction)

A practical, sustainable week built around the principles in `how-to-reduce-a1c.md`. Adjust portions to your calorie needs and check with your doctor before starting.

## Exercise Plan (target ~150 min moderate + 2–3 resistance sessions)

| Day | Activity | Duration | Notes |
|-----|----------|----------|-------|
| Mon | Brisk walk + 10-min post-dinner walk | 30 min | Moderate pace, can talk but not sing |
| Tue | Resistance training (full body) | 30 min | Squats, push-ups, rows, lunges, planks |
| Wed | Brisk walk or cycling | 30 min | Moderate intensity |
| Thu | Resistance training (full body) | 30 min | Same as Tue, add weight/reps as able |
| Fri | Brisk walk + 10-min post-dinner walk | 30 min | Moderate pace |
| Sat | Longer walk / hike / swim | 45–60 min | Enjoyable, keeps it sustainable |
| Sun | Active recovery (stretch, easy walk, yoga) | 20–30 min | Light, mobility focus |

**Tips:** Walk 10–15 min after meals to blunt blood sugar spikes. Rest 1 day between resistance sessions. Build up gradually if you're new to exercise.

## Dietary Plan (sample day structure)

**Breakfast**
- Greek yogurt (plain) + berries + a sprinkle of nuts/seeds, or
- Veggie omelet (2 eggs) + whole-grain toast + avocado

**Lunch**
- Large salad with grilled chicken/fish + olive oil vinaigrette, or
- Grain bowl: quinoa + roasted vegetables + lean protein + beans

**Dinner**
- Half plate non-starchy vegetables, quarter lean protein (chicken, fish, tofu), quarter whole grains (brown rice, quinoa, sweet potato)

**Snacks (optional)**
- Apple + peanut butter, handful of almonds, carrots + hummus, hard-boiled egg

**Drinks**
- Water as primary drink; unsweetened tea/coffee
- Limit or avoid sugary drinks, juice, and sweetened coffee

## Weekly Grocery Focus
- **Proteins:** chicken, fish (salmon, tuna), eggs, tofu, Greek yogurt, beans/lentils
- **Veggies:** leafy greens, broccoli, peppers, cauliflower, zucchini, tomatoes
- **Whole grains:** oats, quinoa, brown rice, whole-grain bread
- **Healthy fats:** olive oil, avocado, nuts, seeds
- **Fruit (in moderation):** berries, apples, citrus

## Foods to Limit
- Sugary drinks, sweets, pastries, white bread/rice/pasta
- Highly processed snacks and fried foods
- Large portions of starchy carbs at once

## Weekly Check-Ins
- Track blood sugar a few times a week to see what works
- Weigh in weekly (same time/conditions)
- Note energy, cravings, and how you feel after meals

---

**Note:** This is general health information, not medical advice. Adjust to your individual needs and confirm with your doctor or a registered dietitian — especially regarding medications, portion sizes, and exercise intensity.
