## Assistant

Florida’s **property-tax Amendment 3 passed** in the November 3, 2026 election:

- **Yes: approximately 62%**
- **No: approximately 38%**
- **Required to pass: 60%**

These are **unofficial results** as of November 4. [Florida Election Watch](https://floridaelectionwatch.gov/CompareStateToCounty/900003)

The amendment increases the homestead exemption for non-school property taxes to **$150,000 in 2027** and **$250,000 in 2028**. [Jacksonville Today](https://jaxtoday.org/2026/11/03/amendment-3-tax-cut-passes/)

**Not all ballots are confirmed counted yet.** Florida’s reporting page shows precincts reporting, but it hasn’t marked all ballot categories complete. “100% of precincts reporting” doesn’t necessarily mean every ballot has been counted. [Florida Election Watch](https://floridaelectionwatch.gov/CountyReportingStatus)
