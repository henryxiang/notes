### Amazon and Intel extend the AI-spending selloff to chips

The full results are in section 7 of the [October 29 note](2026-10-29-market-watch.md). In brief:

- **Amazon** is down about 6.5% (Reuters via [Investing.com](https://ng.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/premarket-movers-intel-amazon-drop-crypto-stocks-slide-after-binance-theft-2770981)) to 7.2% (Reuters, 4:04 AM ET). [Amazon's release](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/news-release/news-release-details/2026/Amazon-com-Announces-Third-Quarter-Results/default.aspx) shows AWS growth accelerated to 39.3%, while trailing free cash flow fell to -$42.4 billion as property and equipment purchases increased; the company kept its approximately $220 billion 2026 cash-capex plan. Reuters says Amazon forecast holiday-quarter sales below expectations. A 7% decline from Thursday's $243.79 close would put the stock near $227.
- **Intel** is down about 23% (FXStreet) to 26% (TipRanks). [Intel's release](https://www.intc.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1786/intel-reports-third-quarter-2026-financial-results) shows a Q4 revenue guide of $15.0 billion to $16.0 billion, about $1.5 billion below consensus at the midpoint, and a non-GAAP gross-margin guide of 38.0%. Reuters says Intel warned it expects to burn cash this year amid manufacturing bottlenecks and weak PC demand. A 25% decline from $92.07 would put the stock near $69.
- **The read-through has reached chips.** AMD is down about 7% to 8%, Broadcom about 4% and Nvidia about 2.5% to 3% (Reuters; TipRanks). Thursday's selling hit Alphabet, software and Amazon but barely touched Nvidia; that distinction is failing this morning. Alphabet is down only about 0.9% after its 12.6% drop (Reuters).
- **Investors are pricing the cost of growth.** Reuters quotes Direxion's Jake Behan: "the market isn't just asking how fast you can grow. It's asking what you have to spend to get there." Reuters says Amazon and Intel are poised to lose about $300 billion in combined market value if losses hold, and that Microsoft raised $25 billion in a debt sale Thursday.
- **Asia felt it first.** Chip and memory names led the Nikkei and Kospi lower; see overseas markets above.
