## 2. What is driving the open

### Anthropic's IPO: pricing above the range, trading from 10:00 AM ET

- **Pricing.** Anthropic priced 980 million Class A shares at $83, above its $70 to $78 range. Of those, 956,351,354 are being sold by the company and 23,648,646 by selling stockholders, with a 30-day option for 147 million additional company shares. Gross public-offering proceeds are about $81.3 billion, the largest IPO on record ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering); [final prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074944/anthropicpbc-424b4.htm)). The prospectus lists 13.22 billion shares outstanding after the offering and concurrent private placement, implying an initial equity value of about $1.10 trillion at $83 before counting options and unvested awards; reported fully diluted estimates are higher.
- **Concurrent placement.** Google and Nvidia each committed $10 billion, AMD $2.5 billion, Broadcom, Micron and Microsoft $1 billion each, and TSMC $500 million to a concurrent private placement of Class N shares at the IPO price. Anthropic estimates combined net proceeds of about $104.8 billion from the public offering and the $26 billion placement ([final prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074944/anthropicpbc-424b4.htm)). These strategic commitments strengthen the funding signal but are not part of the tradable IPO float.
- **Timing.** Nasdaq's system notice said ANT would be released for quotation at 9:50 AM ET and become eligible for trading at approximately 10:00 AM ET ([Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/Trader.aspx?id=MarketSystemStatusToday)). The first trade may come later while the opening auction is built.
- **Demand indications.** NBC cites IG and Hyperliquid derivatives that implied a gain of as much as 27% over the offer price, and TradingKey cites Bloomberg for a gray-market open 20% to 26% above it. These are indications, not trades. NBC also cites Vanda Research's estimate of about $250 million of retail buying at the open; up to 20% of the offering is allocated to retail investors.
- **Read-through.** TradingKey and CNBC attribute part of this morning's gains in chips, storage and optical stocks to the debut. Seagate is up about 5.5%, Western Digital about 4.9% and SanDisk about 4.6%.
- **Our interpretation:** The pricing removes the uncertainty about demand, and the premarket shows no sign of the software selling seen on Tuesday, when investors appeared to raise cash for the deal. The risk now is the reverse: if ANT opens strongly, some money may rotate into it from other AI stocks through the day. Disclosure: this note was prepared with the help of Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic; the IPO coverage here is limited to reported facts.

- **Guidance.** Full-year net sales growth was raised to 4.9% to 5.2% from 4% to 5%, adjusted operating income growth to 8% to 8.6% from 7% to 8.5%, and adjusted EPS to $2.84 to $2.89 from $2.80 to $2.87 (CNBC). After TJX's and Target's stronger comps, Walmart's numbers look steady rather than strong, and fuel costs are a new headwind.
