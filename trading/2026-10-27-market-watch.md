## 2. What is driving the open

### Oil: peace signals vs. Ras Tanura

Iran's supreme leader, Mojtaba Khamenei, reportedly called for pursuing the conditions Iranian officials have set for ending the war. The report is a paraphrase; no direct quote was found. An Iranian ambassador floated a seven-day plan: Hormuz would reopen on day six if Washington accepts Iran's conditions, and talks would start on day seven ([ZeroHedge](https://zerohedge.com/markets/futures-rise-oil-slides-irans-supreme-leader-talks-peace-and-shrugs-ras-tanura-attack-0), [Investrade](https://investrade.com/morning-preview-october-27-2026/)). The two sides remain far apart. Iran wants the U.S. blockade of its ports and the oil sanctions lifted. Vice President JD Vance says Iran must reduce enrichment, and President Trump has said the U.S. may strike again ([FinanceFeeds](https://financefeeds.com/brent-crude-oil-price-114-68-tuesday-fomc-day-one-after-monday-round-trip-tehran-talks-bull-120-bear-108/)).

Supply risk persists. Iranian state media reported missile strikes on Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura complex, and the Houthis claimed the attack. The roughly 550,000-barrel-a-day figure refers to refinery capacity, not the export terminal's throughput. [Reuters, citing Bloomberg](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2637158/saudi-led-coalition-says-it-intercepted-houthi-missiles-drones-attacking-kingdom?amp=1), reported a contained fire near the terminal based on a person familiar with the matter; damage was unclear and Saudi authorities had not commented in that report. This does not establish a confirmed production or loading outage. On Monday, Saudi, Turkish and Pakistani officials met Iranian leaders in Tehran and agreed to keep talking, according to Iranian state media ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-tuesday-october-27-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-081901995.html)). FinanceFeeds reports Hormuz transits of about four on October 18 versus roughly 85 a day before the war, and U.S. strategic reserves at 282.6 million barrels, the lowest since 1982. These figures were not independently verified; the October 18 transit count is nine days old and does not establish current traffic.

On Monday, December Brent settled down 91 cents at $115.81, after briefly topping $117. December WTI settled down $1.51 at $101.76 ([FinanceFeeds, citing Reuters](https://financefeeds.com/brent-crude-oil-price-114-68-tuesday-fomc-day-one-after-monday-round-trip-tehran-talks-bull-120-bear-108/)). Oil is pulling back from six-month highs, but it has not broken down.

### AI trade: Meta upsizes, Nvidia commits $3 billion

Meta has priced the upsized offering. Its [pricing release](https://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2026/Meta-Announces-Pricing-of-Offering-of-Depositary-Shares-Representing-Series-A-Mandatory-Convertible-Preferred-Stock-and-Offering-of-Class-A-Common-Stock-in-Connection-with-Forward-Sale-Agreements/default.aspx) confirms $25 billion of Class A common stock at $625 a share, up from $22.5 billion, plus $12.5 billion of depositary shares at $50 each. Each depositary share represents 1/20 of a 6.50% mandatory convertible preferred share. The $37.5 billion total excludes fees and options; closing is expected October 29, subject to conditions. Meta initially receives no common-stock proceeds; forward settlement is expected by November 19, 2027. Its [October 26 SEC prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000119312526431036/d116268dfwp.htm) describes a separate $60 billion Class C at-the-market program planned for Q1 2027 or later. Including that plan gives $97.5 billion before options; $95 billion was the pre-upsize total. These amounts are not cash already received.

Nvidia said it would invest $3 billion and expand its partnership on Meta's Muse models, including wider use of Vera Rubin systems, according to [Investing.com, syndicated by Yahoo](https://sg.finance.yahoo.com/news/nvidia-invest-3-bln-meta-064611851.html/).
