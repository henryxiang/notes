**Regime:** A rotation-driven, oil- and rate-sensitive market. Technology is trying to recover after Tuesday's late fade, helped by strong AI-linked results from SpaceX and Supermicro.

## 2. What is driving the open

### SpaceX results revive AI enthusiasm

SpaceX reported third-quarter revenue of $14.575 billion, up 161% year over year, versus about $12.7 billion expected. Basic EPS was $0.37 and diluted EPS was $0.35. Its AI segment, now called "Super Intelligence," contributed $7.56 billion, more than the Space and Connectivity segments combined. Shares were up roughly 6% to 8% in early trading ([SpaceX's SEC-filed release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026066482/spaceexplorationtechnologi.htm), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-wednesday-october-21-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-100000105.html)). Musk also said SpaceX and Tesla have formed special committees to discuss a possible merger, although he played down the chance of an imminent deal ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/67064446bf0afdf529db55bdec82af69)). That adds a Tesla-specific catalyst ahead of tonight's report.

Supermicro estimated preliminary fiscal first-quarter revenue of $18.5–19.5 billion, above its prior $14.5–15.5 billion guidance, and estimated a 12.0%–12.5% gross margin versus 10.4%–10.8% previously. Shares rose about 10% premarket ([company release filed with the SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1375365/000137536526000025/smcifirstquarterfy2027busi.htm)). Together these results ease Tuesday's debt-funded AI-spending worries, though Tuesday's CoreWeave and credit concerns have not gone away.

### Oil and the Middle East stay in focus

Brent is back near $110 as the Iran talks drag on, reversing part of Tuesday's diplomacy-driven drop to $108.79. Higher oil keeps inflation risk alive and limits how far yields can fall ([Benzinga](https://benzinga.com/markets/equities/26/10/62488591/stock-market-today-sp-500-nasdaq-100-futures-gains-as-iran-prolongs-us-talks-ahead-of-midterms-nflx-spcx-smci-in-focus)). The weekly crude inventory report is due at 10:30 AM ET.

### Rates

The 10-year yield is about 5.10% to 5.12%, unchanged to slightly lower. The $13 billion 20-year reopening at 1:00 PM ET is the day's main rates test; the prior 20-year auction stopped at 5.42%. That prior stop is context, not the correct benchmark for today's demand: the more useful real-time tests are the stop versus the when-issued yield, bid-to-cover ratio and bidder mix. The [Treasury schedule](https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/221/TentativeAuctionScheduleQ22026.pdf) confirms today's reopening, and the [auction announcement details](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/events/econoday/692949) confirm the size and 1:00 PM competitive-bid deadline. There are no major U.S. economic releases before the open.
