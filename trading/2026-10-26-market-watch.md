## 2. What is driving the open

### Oil eases on a quiet weekend for U.S.–Iran escalation

President Trump said there would be no strike on Iran on Sunday and framed the choice as making a deal or "finishing them off," leaving military action open ([Pakistan Today](https://www.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/10/25/trump-rules-out-iran-strike-for-today-weighs-deal-against-further-military-action)). Qatari mediators traveled to Tehran in a U.S.-coordinated effort to ease tensions ([Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/qatari-mediators-in-tehran-in-us-coordinated-effort-to-ease-tensions-official-says/), [Reuters via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/us-stock-futures-edge-oil-100215774.html)), and Reuters sources said Saudi, Turkish and Pakistani officials were expected in Tehran on Monday ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/investinglive-asia-pacific-market-news-brazil-etf-slumps-on-lula-win/)).

The supply risk has not gone away. The Houthis claimed weekend attacks on a Saudi airport and oil facilities, and Saudi Arabia said it intercepted more missiles and drones on Sunday ([HDFC Sky](https://hdfcsky.com/news/brent-crude-oil-price-today-october-26-2026-brent-falls-2-1percent-to-114-3-wti-drops-2-2percent-to-101-even-as-attacks-continue-on-saudi)). Brent rose about 6% last week. Friday's oil surge, with December Brent settling at $116.72 and December WTI at $103.27, also pushed the Dow down more than 600 points. Monday's 2% pullback is relief, not a reversal.

### Brazil: Lula's win triggers a sharp offshore selloff

Lula beat Flávio Bolsonaro in Sunday's runoff by about 51.8% to 48.2% of valid votes, according to [Rio Times' count of electoral-court figures](https://www.riotimesonline.com/brazil-election-results-2026/). The draft’s claim that Flávio had not conceded could not be corroborated during review. Offshore Brazilian assets fell hard:

- The iShares MSCI Brazil ETF (EWZ) was down almost 26% premarket, according to [Reuters via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/us-stock-futures-edge-oil-100215774.html). Yahoo's live blog says that, if sustained, it would be EWZ's largest one-day drop since 2000. An undated trending-ticker box on the same page showed EWZ up 1.77%, which conflicts and is not used.
- The November real future fell about 9%, and CME dollar–real futures rose about 10% on Sunday evening ([Rio Times](https://www.riotimesonline.com/brazil-real-falls-after-lula-runoff-win/)). Thin Sunday-night trading may overstate the move until B3 opens.
- Goldman Sachs' extreme scenario calls for a 10% rise in USD/BRL, about 200 bp higher long-term Brazilian yields and a roughly 15% equity decline ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/investinglive-asia-pacific-market-news-brazil-etf-slumps-on-lula-win/)).

U.S.-listed Brazil names (Petrobras, MercadoLibre, Nu Holdings, XP) are tumbling, according to [Investrade](https://investrade.com/morning-preview-october-26-2026/); I found no reliable percentages. The direct effect on U.S. indexes is small, but the move is a test of emerging-market risk appetite. Chicago soybean futures fell to a two-month low on expectations of closer Brazil–China ties.

### AI funding: Meta raises equity, neoclouds slide

**Meta** launched about $35 billion of concurrent offerings: $22.5 billion of Class A common stock through forward sale agreements and about $12.5 billion of depositary shares representing mandatory convertible preferred stock, with underwriter options. Its [October 26 SEC prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000119312526431036/d116268dfwp.htm) verifies these terms and non-binding interest from Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom in buying up to $5 billion of common stock. Meta initially receives no proceeds from the common-stock sale; forward settlement is expected by November 19, 2027. The separate, planned $60 billion Class C at-the-market program is not expected before Q1 2027 and has no agreement in place. Thus $95 billion describes the launch-stage financing plan, not cash raised. Premarket reports conflict: Reuters had Meta down 1.4%, TokenPost more than 4% lower, and a Bloomberg headline said shares climbed (as summarized in search results). Meta fell about 7% Thursday after its earnings and about 1% Friday. Later upsizing and pricing are covered in the [October 27 note](2026-10-27-market-watch.md).

**Neoclouds** are lower after an Elon Musk post on X about neocloud funding: CoreWeave down about 5.4%, Nebius about 3.9%, IREN and Applied Digital more than 3% ([Reuters via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/us-stock-futures-edge-oil-100215774.html)). Investrade characterizes the post as warning that tighter credit could force failures and consolidation among smaller AI infrastructure firms; I did not see the post itself. Investrade also reports SpaceX secured $40 billion of debt financing for GPUs. CoreWeave's unsecured bonds yielded about 15% on October 16 ([24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/10/16/coreweave-and-nebius-sink-9-as-sentiment-index-closes-in-on-extreme-fear/)), so this is the second credit-related hit to the group this month.

Taken together, the AI trade's financing burden is now visible from megacaps (Meta selling equity) to smaller, debt-funded builders. That sets up Microsoft's and Alphabet's capex commentary Wednesday evening as the week's key AI test.

### Rates and the Fed: hold expected, hawkish tone in play

The 10-year is about 4.99%, after rising 5 bp Friday to just under 5%. The [September 16 FOMC statement](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260916a.htm) confirms a 25 bp increase to a 3.75%–4.00% target range.

Friday's data kept the inflation concern alive. Final October University of Michigan sentiment was 48.0, with one-year inflation expectations at 4.7% (from 4.6%) and five-year at 3.5% (from 3.4%) ([Advisor Perspectives](https://www.advisorperspectives.com/dshort/updates/2026/10/23/consumer-sentiment-at-five-month-low)). Gold fell about 4% last week, briefly below $4,000, as higher energy prices supported higher-for-longer rate expectations ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/investinglive-asia-pacific-market-news-brazil-etf-slumps-on-lula-win/)).

### Bullish confirmation

- Diplomacy headlines from Tehran keep December WTI below about $101 and December Brent below $114, with no new Saudi supply damage.
- The 10-year holds below 5.00% into the 2-year auction, easing pressure on long-duration stocks.
- The S&P 500 reclaims Thursday's 7,663.43, and the Dow and equal-weight S&P recover part of Friday's losses, showing that Friday's oil-driven breadth damage is reversing.
- Meta absorbs its offering without dragging other megacaps, and neocloud weakness stays contained to the smaller, credit-dependent names.

### Bearish confirmation

- New strikes or Saudi attacks lift December Brent back above Friday's $116.72 settle and December WTI above $103.
- The 10-year moves above 5.05%, particularly with a weak 2-year auction or a hot Dallas Fed prices-paid reading.
- The S&P 500 closes below Friday's 7,623.68 and the Nasdaq below 25,955.34.
- Neocloud credit stress spreads to semiconductors and megacaps, or the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index reverses Friday's reported rebound (exact percentage unresolved above).
- The Brazil shock spreads into broader emerging-market selling and a stronger dollar.
- VIX closes above 20.

### Base case

A modestly higher open as oil eases, followed by range trading ahead of Wednesday's FOMC decision and megacap results. Energy may lag on the oil pullback, while Friday's laggards (staples, discretionary, communication services) have room to bounce. The idiosyncratic shocks (Brazil, Meta's offering, neoclouds) are more likely to show up in individual stocks than in the indexes unless they spread to semiconductors or credit. Oil headlines can still reverse the tape quickly; confirm against breadth and the 5.00% level on the 10-year.
