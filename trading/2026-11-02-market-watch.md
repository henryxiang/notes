## 2. What is driving the open

### A relief bounce in chips and AI infrastructure

Friday's 7.2% semiconductor drop was led by Intel's 28.6% loss and spread to AMD, Broadcom, Nvidia, Micron and Dell (see the [October 30 note](2026-10-30-market-watch.md)). This morning some of that is reversing:

- **Memory.** Micron, Sandisk and Western Digital are up about 2% as traders return to AI names, according to [CNBC's premarket movers](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/02/stocks-making-the-biggest-moves-premarket-crwd-rivn-mu.html) (7:42 AM ET). TipRanks' headline feed also lists AMD, Nvidia and Broadcom higher premarket; no percentages were given.
- **AI-infrastructure hardware.** Vertiv is up about 2.5% after a 5% Friday drop and Bloom Energy about 2.3% after an 8% drop (CNBC).
- **Japan's read was mixed.** Kioxia fell more than 15% on its outlook, while chip-equipment makers such as Tokyo Electron rose on earnings (search summary above).
- **Our interpretation:** The bounce is about 0.6% to 0.8% in Nasdaq-100 futures after a 3.46% Friday loss in the cash index. That is a partial retracement, not evidence that the AI-spending concern from Alphabet, Amazon and Intel has been resolved.

### Marvell agrees to buy Lumentum for about $115 billion

[Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/marvell-agrees-115-billion-allstock-deal-to-buy-lumentum-4991232) (7:19 AM) reports the terms:

- **Structure.** All stock: 4.5 Marvell shares for each Lumentum share, or about $1,132 a share and a 23.9% premium to each company's unaffected October 28 close. The implied enterprise value, including debt, is about $115 billion. Marvell holders would own about 67% of the combined company.
- **Timing and synergies.** Expected to close in the first half of 2028, subject to shareholder and regulatory approvals. The companies project more than $1 billion in annual run-rate cost savings and expect the deal to add to Marvell's non-GAAP earnings per share in the first year after closing.
- **Results alongside the deal.** Lumentum's fiscal Q1 adjusted EPS was $4.57 vs. $4.21 expected on $1.26 billion in revenue, and it guided Q2 revenue to $1.575 billion to $1.625 billion vs. $1.48 billion consensus. Marvell said preliminary Q3 revenue is consistent with guidance.
- **Premarket moves conflict.** Investing.com had Lumentum up 2.5% when it was halted at 6:57 AM ET and Marvell up 3.4%; CNBC (7:42 AM ET) had Marvell up about 3%. The AP (updated 8:07 AM) had Marvell down more than 1% and Lumentum down almost 3%. These readings do not establish a price path: article update times are not quote timestamps, so neither stock's latest direction is confirmed.
- **Announcement cross-check.** The [joint announcement on Lumentum’s investor-relations site](https://investor.lumentum.com/financial-news-releases/news-details/2026/Marvell-and-Lumentum-to-Combine-Creating-a-Category-Leader-in-AI-Connectivity/default.aspx) confirms the 4.5-share ratio, $115 billion enterprise value, October 28 unaffected-price basis, ownership split, projected cost savings and expected first-half 2028 closing.
- **Context.** Bloomberg reported talks after the October 28 close; Marvell was down about 13.9% at 2:44 PM ET on October 29 ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/10/29/why-marvell-stock-is-down-today/)). Because the deal is all stock, its value to Lumentum holders moves with Marvell's share price.

### Oil drops about 4% on Iran diplomacy

- **Talks continue in Qatar.** Iranian negotiators were in Qatar over the weekend on a proposal that could reopen the Strait of Hormuz within two days if U.S. conditions are met; Iran says no direct talks with the U.S. are under way (AP). TipRanks says Iran indicated a deal "remains possible" after two days of Qatar-mediated talks.
- **Washington signals patience.** [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/2026/11/02/oil-prices-slide-more-than-3-as-trump-signals-more-time-for-iran-diplomacy/), citing Axios, reports President Trump wants to give diplomacy more time before considering renewed strikes, a stance signaled for after Tuesday's midterms. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said Saturday no decision had been made and that the president prefers a diplomatic resolution. Mr. Trump told CBS, "Either they'll make a deal, or we'll just end it."
- **The supply risk has not gone away.** The National reports two vessels struck over the weekend, including a ship hit by a projectile in the Strait on Sunday (UKMTO), and an LNG tanker attacked Friday. TipRanks says reports suggest both sides are preparing for possible military action after the midterms.
- **Context.** Brent rose about 15% and WTI about 12% in October, to their highest levels since April (The National). UBS sees Brent near $110 at year-end (The National).
- **Energy stocks lag.** Exxon is down about 1.2%, Chevron nearly 1% and Valero about 1.5% premarket (CNBC). Energy was Friday's best sector at +1.91%.
