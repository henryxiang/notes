## 2. What is driving the open

### Huang resigns

- **The announcement.** Nvidia's board announced that Huang "has resigned as President, Chief Executive Officer, and a member of the Board, effective immediately" ([press release, filed as Exhibit 99.1 to an 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000082/nvidiaannouncesceotransiti.htm)). Colette Kress, CFO since 2013, is interim CEO and stays CFO. Harvey C. Jones, former Synopsys CEO, becomes the board's first-ever chair. The board will consider internal and external candidates for a permanent CEO.
- **Earnings stay on schedule.** Nvidia will report fiscal Q3 results on Tuesday, November 17, at about 4:20 PM ET (1:20 PM PT), with the call at 5:00 PM ET. The board says it expects "operations, customer commitments and product roadmap to continue without interruption."
- **Timing.** The 8-K was filed at 8:52 AM ET. [StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/articles/stock-market-today-live-november-12-2026) says the board accepted the resignation on November 11, after the Wall Street Journal reported Wednesday evening that it was preparing to remove him. The release gives no reason for the resignation.
- **The price.** Nvidia is down about 13.4% near $150.50 at 9:27 AM ET, against Wednesday's $173.73 close and below Wednesday's $156.20 after-hours low. It was $155.44 (-10.5%) at 9:04 AM ET, about 15 minutes after the announcement, and kept falling. StockTitan put the market-value loss at about $441 billion at $155.44. Yahoo's live blog says the stock broke below a floor near $164 that had held twice in the past 14 months.
- **Analysts.** Robert Pavlik of Dakota Wealth said Huang's exit could "leave a vacuum at the top" and "they're going to have to find a successor" (Reuters). Yahoo lists a headline saying Bernstein and Oppenheimer downgraded the stock; the details were not confirmed before this note's cutoff.
- **Our interpretation:** The uncertainty has moved from whether Huang stays to who replaces him. A CFO as interim CEO, five days before earnings, puts more weight on what the company says on November 17 about demand and guidance, and on whether a permanent successor is named quickly.

### AI infrastructure gives back Wednesday's rally

- **Nebius.** Q3 revenue rose 571% to $980.8 million, beating estimates, but the company now expects 2026 revenue at the low end of its $3.0 billion to $3.4 billion range, below a FactSet consensus of about $3.35 billion. It raised 2026 capex to approximately $27 billion from a prior $20 billion to $25 billion range and now expects annualized run-rate revenue to exceed $7 billion for February, about two months later than its previous December target ([Nebius shareholder letter](https://assets.nebius.com/assets/5bc733ff-bed7-415c-af10-74ce7881a764/Nebius-SHL-2026-Q3.pdf), [Reuters via Investing.com](https://investing.com/news/stock-market-news/nasdaq-futures-fall-as-nvidia-turmoil-rattles-ai-stocks-5020761)). The stock is down about 22% near $120.30.
- **CoreWeave** is down about 16% near $50.93, giving back most of Wednesday's 24.7% gain. Oracle is down about 4.5%.
- **Our interpretation:** Nebius's results are a second blow to the AI-infrastructure trade on top of Nvidia. Wednesday's rally rested on CoreWeave's demand signal; Nebius shows that the same demand comes with heavy spending and delays in turning it into revenue.

A close below 7,520.93 would mean Wednesday's rally has been fully reversed; a close above 7,577.39 would mean the market is treating Nvidia as a company-specific event.
