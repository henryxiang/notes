## 2. What is driving the open

### CoreWeave beats and raises

- **The results.** CoreWeave reported Q3 revenue of $3.66 billion, up 168%, against a $3.55 billion estimate. It raised its full-year revenue guide to $13.0 billion to $13.3 billion, against an LSEG consensus of $12.86 billion, and raised its adjusted operating income guide to $1.275 billion to $1.375 billion. Backlog was about $132.5 billion ([company release](https://investors.coreweave.com/news/news-details/2026/CoreWeave-Reports-Strong-Third-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx), [StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/articles/stock-market-today-live-november-11-2026)). Adjusted operating income of $379 million compared with a $234 million estimate ([CoinCentral](https://coincentral.com/stock-market-today-nasdaq-futures-jump-as-coreweave-results-ease-ai-demand-worries)).
- **The reaction.** CoreWeave is up about 14% near $55.74, after trading between $53.73 and $57.26. Tuesday evening's reaction was mixed, about +1% to +5% (November 10 note); the move grew overnight.
- **Peers.** Nebius is up about 12% near $150.30 and Iren about 7%. Oracle, another big AI-capacity seller, is up about 5%.
- **Our interpretation:** The market is focusing on demand (revenue and backlog) and setting aside Tuesday evening's concerns about thinner margins and about $1 billion a quarter of interest expense. That fits a market that fell hard last week on AI-spending doubts and was looking for confirmation that demand is holding.

### Chips lead; Nvidia lags

- **Chips.** On Yahoo quotes at about 9:28 AM ET, Micron is up about 7.2%, AMD about 5.8%, Broadcom about 5.4% and TSMC about 4.8%. SMH is up about 4.4%, and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index was up about 4.6% in the opening minutes.

- **Nvidia.** Nvidia is up about 2.5% near $173.03, after a premarket range of $172.50 to $178.36. No new facts on the board's review of Jensen Huang, the China shipments report or the class action were found before this note's cutoff.
- **Our interpretation:** Nvidia fading from $178.36 while other chips hold their gains repeats Tuesday's pattern. The rest of the chip group is trading on AI demand, and Nvidia on its own governance and export-control risk.

Our interpretation: the gap takes the S&P 500 past both Tuesday's high and Monday's high in one move, so some fading is likely. Nvidia's fade in premarket is an early sign of that.
